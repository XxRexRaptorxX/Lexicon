# The first night.

The first night is very dangerous if you are not prepared for it!

I recommend building you into a mountain at dusk and looking for *Ores* in the meantime. 

Use the <ItemLink id="minecraft:coal" /> you find to smelt food and ores and make a *Torches*. 
And use *Iron Ingots* or *Copper Ingots* to craft higher quality *Tools* and *Armor* for you.

But don't dig too deep and watch out for [Monsters](../creatures/monsters.md) in caves! 

A <ItemLink id="minecraft:torch" /> and light in general prevent them from spawning.
